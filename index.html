What the app does

Piano Learning – Playing against Notes is a music-reading trainer. It shows notes on a grand staff and you play them on the on-screen piano keys, with a mouse or computer keyboard, or on a real MIDI piano or keyboard connected by USB. You can choose which keys to practise and take a short Test Skill reaction test of 20 notes with a spoken countdown, on iPhone, iPad and Windows. Everything happens on your device.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, send or store any personal information, and the developer has no servers. The app has no account and no analytics of its own. The only personal information the developer ever receives is what you choose to send when you contact support by email (see Contact). If you donate on Windows, PayPal shows the payment in the developer's PayPal account; the developer does not copy, keep or use those details (see Donations).

The iPhone and iPad app shows ads from Google, described in the next section. Google, not the developer, collects the information needed to show those ads. The Windows app has no ads and does not connect to the internet itself.

Advertising on iPhone and iPad

The free iPhone and iPad app shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner at the top of the screen, which changes by itself every so often, and a full-screen ad after a number of notes played and before each Test Skill run. Until the banner has loaded, the piano keys wait, so the free app needs an internet connection. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • an identifier for your device for this app (the app does not ask for permission to track you, so Google does not receive Apple's advertising identifier);
  • your IP address, used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • how you interact with the ads, such as views and taps;
  • technical, performance and crash information about the ads.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive information that identifies you. Google AdMob is the only third-party service in the iPhone and iPad app. It is bound by Google's privacy policy and AdMob terms to protect this information, and the developer sends it nothing about you.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent message asks for your choices before ads are personalised. You can change them at any time with the Privacy button in the app. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for a general audience.

Remove Ads purchase (iPhone and iPad only)

The Remove Ads / Restore Purchase button lets you buy a one-time upgrade that removes all ads, or restore it on another device. Afterwards the banner box at the top shows “Paid Version” instead of an ad. Apple handles the payment and any refunds. The developer never receives your payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks Apple whether this purchase has been made, and remembers the answer on your device.

The Windows app has no ads and no paid version, so it has no Remove Ads purchase.

Donations (Windows only)

Donations apply only to the Windows app. The iPhone and iPad app has no Donate button: there, the only payment is the Remove Ads purchase through Apple.

The Windows app has an optional Donate button that opens the developer's PayPal donation page in your web browser. Donations are entirely voluntary, as support for the developer's work. A donation does not unlock, change or affect anything in the app: every feature works the same for everyone, whether or not they donate. Donations are not refundable.

The app itself sends nothing to PayPal or the developer and never sees your payment details. PayPal handles the donation under the PayPal Privacy Statement and keeps its own record of the payment, which, as with any PayPal payment, also appears in the developer's PayPal account. The developer does not copy, keep or use donors' PayPal details, keeps no donation records, and never shares or sells anything about donors.

MIDI pianos and keyboards

When you connect a MIDI piano or keyboard, the app reads which keys you press and release so it can check your answers, and it shows the instrument's name (for example "Digital Piano") on screen. This information is used at that moment only. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • The app does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • The piano sound and the right and wrong sounds you hear are generated by the app itself. The spoken “Ready?” and “3, 2, 1” of Test Skill are made on your device by its built-in speech voice (Windows, or iOS and iPadOS). No text or audio leaves your device.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the app uses the internet only to load ads (the banner and the full-screen ads), show the consent message and check the Remove Ads purchase.
  • On Windows, the app runs as a standard desktop app (the Microsoft Store lists this as "Uses all system resources"). It uses this only to play sound, speak the Test Skill countdown with the Windows speech voice and read a connected MIDI instrument. It doesn't connect to the internet itself: the links in the About card and the Donate button open in your web browser.

The Microsoft Store and the App Store

You download the app from the Microsoft Store or Apple's App Store. Microsoft and Apple handle downloads, purchases (including Remove Ads) and updates under their own privacy policies. They may give the developer summary statistics, such as how many people installed the app, and crash information if you have agreed to share it with developers in your device settings. This information does not identify you to the developer.

Your choices and your data

  • On your device: everything the app keeps (your settings, your best Test Skill score and the purchase status) is deleted when you uninstall the app. The developer holds none of it.
  • Ads on iPhone and iPad: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you can change or withdraw your ad choices at any time with the Privacy button in the app. You can also manage Google's ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. To ask Google about information it holds, use the contact details in the Google Privacy Policy. Buying Remove Ads stops all ads.
  • Emails to the developer: your messages are kept only as long as needed to help you. Ask at any time and the developer will delete them.
  • Donations (Windows): the developer keeps no donors' details or donation records, so there is nothing to delete. PayPal's own record of a payment is covered by the PayPal Privacy Statement; ask PayPal about it.
  • Your rights: if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can ask what the developer holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed specifically at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone. On Windows there are no ads, and donating is never needed to use any feature. On iPhone and iPad, ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience, and buying Remove Ads switches them off completely.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, see the support page for the developer's email address. If you write, the developer receives your email address and your message, uses them only to reply, keeps them only as long as needed, and does not share or sell them.
