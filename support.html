Quick answers

The iPhone and iPad app and the Windows app work a little differently. Pick your version below.

iPhone and iPad

How do I remove the ads?

Tap Remove Ads / Restore Purchase (top right, on the second row). Read the details, tap Continue to Payment and confirm with Apple. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it works on every iPhone and iPad signed in with the same Apple ID.

I bought Remove Ads, but I still see ads.

Tap Remove Ads / Restore Purchase again, or Already bought it? Restore in the pop-up. Make sure you are online and signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If it still does not work, email me.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all purchases and refunds. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What do L Only, R Only, Basic and Expert mean?

L Only is for your left hand: one white-key note from C3 to B3 on the bass staff. R Only is for your right hand: one white-key note from C4 to B4 on the treble staff. Basic asks for one note anywhere on the piano. Expert asks for one to three notes at once.

Can I play on a real piano or keyboard?

Yes, with a USB MIDI piano or keyboard. Connect it with the right cable or adapter for your device (a USB-C cable for USB-C devices, a Lightning-to-USB camera adapter for Lightning devices). A green MIDI chip appears next to your score. Tap it to switch the app's own sound for the piano keys on or off.

Windows

Is the Windows app free?

Yes. It has no ads and nothing to buy, and every feature works for everyone.

What does the Donate button do?

It opens my PayPal donation page in your web browser. Donating is completely voluntary, as support for my work, and it does not unlock or change anything in the app. Donations are not refundable. Donations are only for the Windows app: on iPhone and iPad the only payment is Remove Ads.

What do L Only, R Only, Basic and Expert mean?

L Only asks for one white-key note on the bass staff and R Only one white-key note on the treble staff. Basic asks for one note at a time. All three use only the keys you choose in the From and To boxes. Expert asks for one to three notes at once, anywhere on the piano.

How do the From and To boxes work?

They set the lowest and highest key for L Only, R Only and Basic, anywhere from A0 to C8. The range must hold at least 7 keys (black keys count too); if it doesn't, a message tells you which keys would work. The playable keys jump to your range straight away. The app starts at C4 to A4 every time you open it. In Expert the boxes are greyed out.

How does Test Skill work?

Click Test Skill beside the staff. After “Ready?” and a spoken 3, 2, 1 countdown, 10 notes appear one at a time, from the same keys as your level (all 88 keys in Expert). You have 2 seconds for each. The right key brings the next note at once; a wrong key doesn't. At the end you see how many you got right and your average time. Click OK to go back, or Cancel (or Esc) at any time to stop a test.

How is my best score kept?

Your best test is shown below the Test Skill button and kept on your PC, even after you close the app, until a test beats it: more right keys wins, and with the same number of right keys the faster average wins. A test needs at least one right key to count. The best score is removed only if you uninstall the app.

I don't hear “Ready?” or the countdown.

Check that your sound is on and turned up. The voice comes from the speech voice built into Windows. If your PC has none, the app plays a short piano note for each step instead.

Can I play on a real piano or keyboard?

Yes. Connect the piano's USB to Host port to your PC with a USB cable (pianos with only round 5-pin MIDI sockets need a USB-MIDI interface cable). Windows needs no driver or setup. A green MIDI chip appears next to your score; click it to switch the app's own sound for the piano keys on or off. If the chip doesn't appear, close other music programs: on Windows a MIDI device can usually be used by only one program at a time.

Both versions

Why is the Sharps button greyed out?

Sharps only work in Basic and Expert. L Only and R Only use white keys only, so the button is switched off there. Your choice comes back when you return to Basic or Expert. On Windows it is also greyed out while a Test Skill run is going.

How do I move along the piano?

Swipe or drag the playable keys sideways, use the arrow buttons, or scroll the small piano above them (on Windows also with the mouse wheel). The + and − buttons zoom in and out, from 3 keys to the whole 88-key piano.

The app asks me to turn my device sideways.

The piano needs a wide screen. Hold your iPhone, iPad or Windows tablet in landscape. On a PC, make the window wider than it is tall.
